# Models

`lucy.nm` (and `lucy4m.nm`, the Lucy forest's 4M-triangle copy) is derived from **Lucy**, from [The Stanford 3D Scanning Repository](http://graphics.stanford.edu/data/3Dscanrep/)
(source: Stanford University Computer Graphics Laboratory), decimated from 28M to 1M (4M) triangles with its cluster DAG
(`tools/nanite-model.ts`). It is not covered by this project's MIT license; Stanford's terms apply:

- free for research and other non-commercial use, and to mirror or redistribute for free, with credit to the
  Stanford Computer Graphics Laboratory;
- not for commercial use, nor in a product for sale;
- don't animate or morph Lucy, don't apply Boolean operations to it, and keep its uses in good taste.
